01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Turning environmental data into useful predictions.

Solar power generation is influenced by several factors, including weather conditions, location and time. Because these factors change throughout the day and across seasons, solar generation can vary significantly.

This project develops a machine-learning system that learns patterns from historical solar generation and weather data. The processed data is used to train a regression model capable of estimating solar power generation for given environmental and temporal conditions.
